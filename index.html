How it works

  1. Type a number — results appear instantly as you type, no button needed.
  2. Get two formats — Nepali Unicode words and English words side by side.
  3. Copy either result with one tap for cheques, invoices or documents.

Uses the Nepali numbering system: हजार (thousand) → लाख (lakh) → करोड (crore) → अरब (arab) → खरब (kharab). Decimals become पैसा (paisa).

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert a number to words in Nepali?

Type the number into the converter box above. It instantly shows the amount in Nepali Unicode words — for example, 12345 becomes बाह्र हजार तीन सय पैंतालिस रुपैंया.

Does it support lakh, crore, arab and kharab?

Yes. The converter follows the Nepali/South-Asian system — thousand (हजार), lakh (लाख), crore (करोड), arab (अरब) and kharab (खरब) — supporting numbers up to 13 digits.

Can it convert decimal amounts with paisa?

Yes. Up to two decimal places are converted to paisa — 123.45 becomes एक सय तेइस रुपैंया, पैंतालिस पैसा.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. This is an installable PWA. Tap “Install App” (or use your browser’s install option) and it works fully offline after the first visit.
